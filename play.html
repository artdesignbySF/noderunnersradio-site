Listen anywhere

The radio plays in many apps. The sound is the same everywhere.

  1. Radio apps
    Search Noderunners Radio in myTuner, TuneIn or radio.net (any phone or tablet), or in RadioDroid and Transistor on Android. Soon also in Radio Garden.
  2. Podcast apps
    In Fountain and other podcast apps with live shows, search Noderunners Radio or add the feed below. In Fountain, a boost sends sats straight to the station.
  3. In the car
    Android Auto and Apple CarPlay: open myTuner, TuneIn or radio.net and search for the station. Soon also through Radio Garden.
  4. Smart speaker soon
    Say: “Alexa, play Noderunners Radio.” Speakers and Sonos usually play radio through TuneIn, radio.net or myTuner.
  5. Smart TV
    Open the myTuner or radio.net app on your TV and search for the station.
  6. Any music player
    Paste the stream link below into VLC (Media → Open Network Stream), Winamp, foobar2000 or a browser tab.
  7. Mobile data
    About 86 MB per hour.
  8. Requests work from every app
    The apps only play the sound. To pick a song, use the jukebox: the website, Telegram, Nostr or X (see above). Your song then plays in every app. Apps can run a few seconds behind each other.

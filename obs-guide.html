OBS Operator Guide

For whoever runs the streaming machine. You already have the stream URL and key — those don't change. Only the scene does.

What changed, in one breath

All on-screen graphics — tonight's DJ lineup, the donation QR, the live "+sats" popups — now come from the station, through one browser source. Your scene should be nothing but the camera. No more baked-in banners, no more pasted-in QR images, no chat panel.

The old Wallet of Satoshi QR must go. Sats scanned from it land in the old wallet and skip tonight's DJ pot — the DJs would literally not get paid their share of those. Delete that image source.

Rebuild the scene (2 minutes)

  1. Remove from your scene: the old top banner, the Wallet of Satoshi QR image, the Telegram chat capture — every graphic that isn't the camera.
  2. Camera full-frame: your cam/set video source stretched to fill the whole canvas.
  3. Add the overlay: Sources → + → Browser → name it NR overlay → set:
    URL: https://noderunnersradio.com/overlay
    Width: 1920   Height: 1080
    Leave the rest on defaults. OK.
  4. Order matters: in the Sources list, drag NR overlay above the camera (top of the list = front of the picture).
  5. If the overlay doesn't fill the canvas exactly: right-click it → Transform → Fit to screen.

What you should see

ImmediatelyThe ⚡ tip QR appears top-right over your camera. Background stays your video — the overlay is transparent.
When the captain opens the sessionThe DJ lineup row appears bottom-center (name discs). If it's not there yet, the session just isn't opened — not your problem.
When someone donatesA big "+N sats ⚡" popup with the donor's name/message, the DJ discs pulse, a current runs along the bottom. All automatic.

During the show

  • Nothing to operate. Popups, lineup, QR — all remote-controlled by the station.
  • If the overlay ever looks stuck or the station says "we pushed an update": right-click the NR overlay source → Refresh cache of current page. Two seconds, no stream interruption.
  • Stream out exactly as you always have — same URL, same key, same encoder settings that worked before.

Hosts: your own controls

Two things no longer need the captain:

  1. Highlight who is playing. Log into your DJ portal. Host accounts see an ON DECK card. Tap the DJ who takes the decks; tap again to unmark. The overlay follows within ~10 seconds. This changes the picture only — it never moves sats.
  2. Move the overlay around. Your venue has its own overlay URL, printed on your portal's YOUR OVERLAY card. Open the drag editor link there, drag the pieces, press SAVE. Your host login is enough. OBS picks up new positions within ~10 seconds — no source refresh needed for positions (styling changes still need one).

If SAVE answers “host login required”, flip HOST MODE on in your portal first, or simply log in again — portal logins last 90 days.

If something's off

  • No overlay visible at all → check the URL is exactly https://noderunnersradio.com/overlay (https, no ?demo), size 1920×1080, source at the top of the list.
  • White or dark box instead of transparency → you may have typed ?demo=1 — that's the preview mode with a fake background. Remove it for the live scene.
  • No lineup row → the captain hasn't opened the session yet, or just did (it refreshes within ~15 s).
  • Anything else → ping the Telegram group; the captain has eyes on the machine.

Disclaimer
Anything this station or our (guest) DJs say and broadcast is open to interpretation — art is in the eye of the beholder. Take it all with a grain of salt or two while listening to Noderunners Radio. Noderunners Radio does its best to log every track played — by us or by guest DJs — for proper logging. Human and machine errors in said log are possible; treat it as a good-faith record, not a guarantee. We do not normalize loudness. Music has dynamics — hushed passages, sudden drops, live sets that swing — and we leave them exactly as the artists made them. Keep a hand near the volume and set it as you see fit.
Signed, from a shipwreck, partly ashore, partly floating in international waters — Noderunners Radio
This site sets no cookies for listeners. The page only remembers what you choose, in your own browser: your backdrop, your own picture and your language. Crew who log in get one session cookie. Our network provider, Cloudflare, may add a security cookie to keep bots out.

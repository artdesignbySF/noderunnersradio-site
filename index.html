Disclaimer
Anything this station or our (guest) DJs say and broadcast is open to interpretation — art is in the eye of the beholder. Take it all with a grain of salt or two while listening to Noderunners Radio. Noderunners Radio does its best to log every track played — by us or by guest DJs. Human and machine errors in that log are possible; treat it as a good-faith record, not a guarantee. We even out loudness between songs: a song much louder than the rest is turned down, never pushed up. Inside a song nothing is squashed — hushed passages and sudden drops stay exactly as the artists made them. Live sets by guest DJs come through as they play them, so keep a hand near the volume.

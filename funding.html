The number on the homepage

The "sats raised" counter is two things added together:

measured sats
Every donation and jukebox payment that has landed in the station's wallets since our Lightning era began (July 2026) — counted from real payment records, never faked, never rounded up. Payouts to DJs don't lower it: raised is raised.

+ 3,000,000 sats baseline
A good-faith estimate of everything raised in the years before we could measure — the Geyser fund era and years of direct support. It's honest accounting from the captain's end, with room for human error. Treat it as a weathered logbook, not a bank statement.

Where the sats go

During a live session, donations pool into a session pot. When the session ends, the guest-DJ share is split evenly across that night's crew — whole sats only, paid straight to each DJ, remainders stay with the ship. The split ladder and where it's heading long-term live on the roadmap.

The jukebox's 21-sat request fee for songs from the ship's own library is purely anti-spam. A request from the Wavlake lane costs 210 sats and is value-for-value, live since July 2026: the full 210 sats leave the station's passthrough wallet through Wavlake to the artist; the ship keeps none of it. A request sent through our bot on X costs the X share — 100 sats today — on top of the artist's 210 on the Wavlake lane; that share exists because every post on X by @JukeboxLNDbot costs the station real money, and it covers X's bill and nothing else. The price may change according to bitcoin's volatility. The road towards more value-for-value is in the manifesto.

We will use your donations and the anti-spam fees at our discretion to keep the lights on, and to maintain and replace hardware as needed. Thank you for your support. We will spend your hard-earned sats wisely and frugally. The "of which remain" figure under the counter on the homepage is what is actually still in the offline jar and the station wallets together, kept up to date by hand by the captain.

Self-custody, always

The station is not a bank. Guest DJs are nudged — loudly — to set their own Lightning Address so payouts never even park here, or to drain their station wallet after every session. Sats belong in wallets their owners control.

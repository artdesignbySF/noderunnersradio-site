Straight with you

Our ship, recently named "The Dread Node", has been crewed by a few rag-tag plebs these past years, proper Noderunners, one and all! However, right now she's taking on water — main speakers blown, studio half-apart. The good news: we keep on keeping on! The bigger early cut isn't a house tax, it's a bilge pump that will help us bootstrap from the wreckage of the first iteration of this project. It steps down in the DJs' favour as we refloat, and the station's real living is meant to come from the music catalogue over time — not from skimming live performers, nor musicians in general! The goal is everyone flying, not just the captain.

The jukebox's ladder

  • Today: the 21-sat request fee is purely anti-spam — it keeps the queue honest and the station's gear running.
  • In progress: direct value-for-value — request a track, and the lion's share (79%) goes straight to the original artists via Lightning. The plumbing is built; the final connection to the artist-payout network is being wired.
  • Always: the background playlist stays properly licensed — the collecting societies get paid for every logged play.

Now / Next / Later

  • Now: live sessions running in the open with automatic honest payouts · DJ quarters with self-custody payouts · the jukebox on the website, Telegram, Nostr and X · the artist-payout lane (Wavlake) · an honest rotating counter · transparency pages like this one.
  • Next: a top-requests chart (the jukebox's most-requested songs, by week, by month and of all time) · hosts & a session calendar (any DJ can raise their hand to host) · DJ & host stat pages.
  • Later: the pro era — real broadcast dreams (FM/AM), paid live effects for the crowd, an installable app.

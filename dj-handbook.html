Guest DJ Handbook

Come aboard, plug in, and get your fair cut of the sats — the plain-language guide to spinning a live set aboard The Dread Node.

— The Dread Node · broadcasting from international waters —
⚓ Fresh rigging — live, but young. Everything here works and has been tested with real sats, but we're actively building: expect rough edges, and the numbers below can still shift while the ship is being rebuilt.

Here's what being a guest DJ on Noderunners gets you:

◆Your own Lightning payout — a wallet and a Lightning Address that's yours alone.
◆A live share of the tips — while you're on air, a slice of every listener donation flows straight to that wallet, automatically.
◆A slot on the schedule — and a berth in the crew.
01

Get your payout wallet

This is where your sats will land. Takes about a minute — no bank, no sign-up forms, no ID.

Set up your Lightning wallet~1 min
  1. Go to noderunnersradio.com/registerOn any phone or laptop browser. No wallet needed beforehand — no email, no KYC, nothing.
  2. Pick your DJ name + a passphraseThe name your listeners know you by — set in stone, one DJ = one name. The passphrase (min 12 characters, 4 random words work great) is your login. You also get a one-time recovery code — write it down somewhere safe; it's your way back in if the passphrase ever goes overboard.
  3. The station mints your walletYou instantly get a Lightning wallet at the station and a Lightning Address (your own you@… that can receive sats from anywhere).
  4. Log in at noderunnersradio.com/login — your quartersSee the sats you've earned, open your station wallet, upload & crop your avatar, write your blurb — and you get your own page at noderunnersradio.com/YourName.
  5. Best move: set your OWN payout addressGot your own wallet (Wallet of Satoshi, Phoenix, your own node…)? Paste its Lightning Address in your quarters and session payouts fly straight to you — never parking at the station. The station is not a bank; self-custody, always.
We will never ask for your wallet password, seed words, or admin key — not once, not ever. If anyone claiming to be the station asks for them, it isn't us.

03

Go live

Stream in

The station broadcasts video through Owncast (you send us your feed from OBS). When your slot's approved we hand you a private stream key and the exact OBS settings — one-to-one, so this part we walk through with you directly.

Keep your scene simple: your camera/set filling the screen. No baked-in banners or QR images — the station's graphics (tonight's lineup, the tip QR, live donation popups) ride on top through one browser-source link we give you.

Gear check, levels, and the stream key all get sorted with you before you go on. No guesswork, no going live cold.

05

Fine print & FAQ

When do I actually get paid?
At the end of the session, automatically. Donations pool up while the lineup plays; when the session closes, the pot settles and your share lands in your station wallet in one fee-free payment. Withdraw whenever you like. This is value-for-value, not a salary: no tips means nothing to split.
Do you ever see my keys or password?
No. Your wallet is yours. The station only needs your public Lightning Address to route your share of the tips — never anything that could spend your sats.
What counts as "my set"?
The window you're booked and live on air. The station switches your split on when you start and off when you wrap.
Can the percentage change on me?
Only in your favour. It steps down for the station as the studio heals, following the ladder above. Whatever the current rate is, it'll always be posted right here.
Do I need Telegram?
Yes, for now — it's where your slot gets confirmed and where the crew coordinates. A free account with an @username set is enough (the slot form shows you how), plus joining t.me/noderunnersradio — and when you join, answer the bouncer bot's are-you-human check or you'll be kicked. Nostr is welcome too, but optional.
I'm not technical — is this going to be hard?
No. Making the wallet is one page and one name. Going live we set up with you personally. If you can run OBS, you can DJ here.
